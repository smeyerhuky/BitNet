# 2026-11-21 P1 Baseline

**Did.** Built 12 seed coding conversations, each with a reference solution that passes its behavior and constraint tests. Ran the baseline three times per conversation at full context: 36 runs, 18,437 tokens spent.

**Found.**
- Behavior and constraint pass rates are both 36 of 36. The corpus is at ceiling, so it cannot show a difference between two conditions.
- The mean prompt is about 380 tokens. That is far too short to test compression. A compactor has almost nothing to remove.
- Results are consistent across the three runs of each conversation.

**Consequence.** The corpus has to be made harder and longer before it can measure anything. Planned changes: histories of about 3,000 to 6,000 tokens; the planted fact placed early in the history and needed later; dependencies between turns; and tasks that fail more often at baseline. Floor results on the current corpus are reported as ceiling-limited and are not used for decisions.
