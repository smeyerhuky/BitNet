# Wiki Article Template

```yaml
---
type: "Concept"
title: "..."
description: "one sentence"
resource: "path or URL the claim comes from"
tags: ["..."]
timestamp: "YYYY-MM-DD"
---
```

Body: a one-idea article with real content, links to related articles, and a source line. Do not add `okf_version` here; only the root `index.md` has it.
